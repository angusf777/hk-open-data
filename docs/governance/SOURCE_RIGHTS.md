# Source Rights and Evidence

HK Open Data catalogues factual metadata and links to authoritative providers. It does not grant rights to upstream APIs, datasets, documentation, names or marks. Upstream terms always control.

## Evidence states

- `not-reviewed`: no current project review of provider terms is recorded.
- `official-terms-linked`: an authoritative terms page is linked, without a project conclusion about a proposed use.
- `restriction-identified`: an explicit provider restriction relevant to users is recorded and linked.
- `ambiguity-identified`: available material leaves a material question unresolved or points to separate terms.
- `provider-confirmation-recorded`: a dated, attributable provider statement is recorded; its scope and wording still control.

Every state includes a checked date. Records may also include the authoritative terms URL, stated attribution, explicit restrictions, separate dataset terms and a concise bilingual evidence note.

## Acceptable notes

Notes may state observable facts such as authentication method, documented rate limit, attribution wording, explicit prohibition, terms URL, document version and verification date. Quote only the minimum necessary wording and link the original.

## Claims this project does not make

Do not turn evidence into categorical permission, legal-clearance or risk-free-use labels. Do not infer dataset rights from a platform's general terms or infer production approval from a reachable endpoint.

## Reverification

Mark evidence stale when its review date exceeds the project threshold or the provider page changes. A broken link creates a visible finding; it does not silently remove the resource or rewrite the evidence state. Material changes require human review.

## Operator responsibility

Users running connectors must verify the current provider terms, intended purpose, caching/retention behavior, attribution and applicable law. Professional advice may be appropriate. The project notice and Apache License 2.0 do not replace that assessment.
