# Source terms and permissions

HK Open Data catalogues factual metadata and links to authoritative providers. It does not grant
rights to third-party APIs, datasets, documentation, names, or marks. The provider's current terms
always control.

## Terms-review states

- `not-reviewed`: no current project review of provider terms is recorded.
- `official-terms-linked`: an authoritative terms page is linked, without a project conclusion about a proposed use.
- `restriction-identified`: an explicit provider restriction relevant to users is recorded and linked.
- `ambiguity-identified`: available material leaves a material question unresolved or points to separate terms.
- `provider-confirmation-recorded`: a dated, attributable provider statement is recorded; its scope and wording still control.

Every state includes a checked date. Records may also include the authoritative terms URL, stated
attribution, explicit restrictions, separate dataset terms, and a concise bilingual review note.

## What a review note may say

Notes may state observable facts such as authentication method, documented rate limit, attribution wording, explicit prohibition, terms URL, document version and verification date. Quote only the minimum necessary wording and link the original.

## Claims this project does not make

Do not turn a terms review into categorical permission, legal-clearance, or risk-free-use labels. Do
not infer dataset rights from a platform's general terms or treat a working endpoint as permission
to use it in production.

## Reverification

Mark a review stale when its date exceeds the project threshold or the provider page changes. A
broken link creates a visible finding; it does not silently remove the resource or rewrite the
review state. Material changes require human review.

## Your responsibility

If you run a connector, verify the provider's current terms, intended purpose, caching and retention
behaviour, attribution requirements, and applicable law. Professional advice may be appropriate.
The project notice and Apache License 2.0 do not replace that assessment.
