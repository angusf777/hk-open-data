# Roadmap

The roadmap is directional, not a promise of delivery, source availability, or provider permission.

## Now

- Publish and maintain the deterministic bilingual catalogue.
- Ship metadata-only releases with checksums and a dependency SBOM.
- Keep the static catalogue accessible and free from automatic requests to listed providers.
- Keep every optional data connection switched off by default.

## Next

- Replace seeded Traditional Chinese summaries with clearly recorded human-reviewed translations.
- Add small connectors backed by synthetic test data where the source's current terms support the
  intended use.
- Expand automated link-health evidence without mutating catalogue truth.
- Improve SDK examples and troubleshooting for people running the toolkit.

## Later

- Community-maintained category guides and reproducible catalogue snapshots.
- Additional self-hosted observability adapters and local export formats.
- A centrally hosted API, proxy, MCP endpoint, source-data cache, or commercial service is **not
  planned**. Any future proposal would require a fresh review of source terms, privacy, security,
  and architecture.
