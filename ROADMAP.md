# Roadmap

The roadmap is directional, not a promise of delivery, source availability, or provider permission.

## Now

- Publish and maintain the deterministic bilingual catalogue.
- Publish permanent source, dataset, and category pages with shareable filters.
- Ship JSON, CSV, SQLite, DCAT, and release metadata with checksums and a dependency SBOM.
- Keep evidence-backed quickstarts and bounded provider-resource checks current.
- Keep the static catalogue accessible and free from automatic requests to listed providers.
- Keep every optional data connection switched off by default.

## Next

- Add small connectors backed by synthetic test data where the source's current terms support the
  intended use.
- Expand automated evidence to more direct resources without treating pages as payload success.
- Add more task-oriented quickstarts and parsing examples for common public-data formats.
- Improve SDK troubleshooting for people running the toolkit.

## Later

- Community-maintained category guides and domain-specific starter projects.
- Additional self-hosted health-check adapters where source terms and maintenance capacity allow.
- A centrally hosted API, proxy, MCP endpoint, source-data cache, or commercial service is **not
  planned**. Any future proposal would require a fresh review of source terms, privacy, security,
  and architecture.
