# Roadmap

The roadmap is directional, not a promise of delivery, source availability, or upstream permission.

## Now

- Publish and maintain the deterministic bilingual catalogue.
- Ship metadata-only releases with checksums and a dependency SBOM.
- Keep the static catalogue provider-traffic-free and accessible.
- Keep all P01/P14 source definitions inactive by default.

## Next

- Replace seeded Traditional Chinese summaries with clearly recorded human-reviewed translations.
- Add small, synthetic-fixture-backed connectors where source-specific evidence supports the use.
- Expand automated link-health evidence without mutating catalogue truth.
- Improve SDK examples and local operator diagnostics.

## Later

- Community-maintained category guides and reproducible catalogue snapshots.
- Additional self-hosted observability adapters and local export formats.
- A centrally hosted API, proxy, MCP endpoint, raw-data cache, or commercial service is **not
  planned without a new rights, privacy, security, and architecture review**.
